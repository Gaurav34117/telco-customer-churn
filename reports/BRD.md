
# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## Project Title

Telco Customer Churn Prediction

## Business Problem

Telecommunication companies invest a significant amount of resources in acquiring new customers. However, retaining existing customers is equally important because customer loss directly impacts company revenue.

Many customers discontinue their services due to various reasons such as high monthly charges, poor service quality, or better offers from competitors. Identifying these customers before they leave allows companies to take appropriate actions to improve customer retention.

## Business Goal

The primary goal of this project is to develop a predictive model that can identify customers who are likely to churn.

The prediction can help telecom companies:

- Reduce customer loss.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Design personalized retention strategies.
- Increase long-term revenue.

---

## Project Scope

This project focuses on analyzing historical customer data and building a machine learning model for churn prediction.

The work includes:

- Data preprocessing
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Feature selection
- Model training
- Performance evaluation

The project does not include deployment or integration into a live telecom system.

## Expected Benefits

The project can help telecom companies identify customers who may discontinue their services. Early prediction enables businesses to take preventive actions such as offering discounts, improving customer support, or providing customized plans, which may reduce customer churn and improve customer retention.

## Conclusion

Predicting customer churn is an important business application of machine learning. This project combines data analysis with predictive modeling to support better business decisions and improve customer retention strategies.
